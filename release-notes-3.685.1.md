# TRG Prime 3.685.1

7 November 2026. The core half of the findings from Eldritch Arts 0.10.0's live test (the module's `docs/LIVE_TESTING_REPORT_0.10.0.md`), which Graham asked to have fixed before Block 8. Two wording fixes for every world, and three seams Eldritch Arts 0.10.1 uses. Built on 3.685.0.

- **Install over 3.685.0.** No migration, and no world data changes. Nothing rewrites an owned item at load.
- **Eldritch Arts 0.10.1 needs it.** Eldritch Arts 0.10.0 and Inner Tempest 1.0.4 run on it unchanged: each seam is default-off.

## For every world

**A rider in place of the weapon's damage is carded as such** (live finding 2: "Spirit Arrow's cast card says "+6d6 piercing on your next ranged Strike". The damage is flat 6d6 in place of the arrow's, not added to it.")
- **Before:** the cast card's line for every strike rider (`riderCastLine`) read "+*dice type* on your next … Strike". It did not read 3.685.0's `replacesDamage` or `flat`, so Spirit Arrow's card promised 6d6 on top of the arrow's damage. The Strike's own damage card was already right.
- **Now:** a replacing rider reads "your next ranged Strike deals **6d6 piercing** in place of its damage, with nothing added" when it is `flat`, and "… in place of its damage (its other additions still apply)" when it is not. A rider that lasts says "every … Strike with it deals …". Every other rider reads as before.

**An unknown recurring type is named in the effect** (live finding 4: "It reads "Recurring Damage 16", while force reads "Recurring Damage 24 (Force)". The flat-check cards name both types correctly.")
- **Before:** the condition's effect name (`conditionEffectName`) added the type only for a type in the system's damage table. Eaog Balls' coldfire read "Recurring Damage 16".
- **Now:** "Recurring Damage 16 (Coldfire)": the type's own name, capitalised, by the same `recurringTypeLabel` 3.685.0 gave the end-phase card. A known type reads as before ("(Force)", "(Fire)"); a condition with no type still has none.
- **A creature already carrying coldfire** keeps its old name until the value next changes, when the name is written again.

## New for modules

Each seam is additive and default-off: a spell, row or effect that does not use the new shape behaves exactly as it did.

| **1** | **A teleport later than its cast** (live finding 1) | `registerDeferredTeleport(key, defers, moduleId)`. `defers({ actor, spell, spellData })` is asked when a spell with the Teleportation trait is cast, and returns `true` when the spell moves nobody yet. | **2** | **A spell aimed at an object, not a creature** (live finding 3) | `flags["trg-prime"].targetsObject = true` on the spell, declared as `selfApplying` is (the printed line is not parsed for "object": "1 creature or object" still wants a creature). `spellNeedsTarget(spellSystem, spell)` is false for it, so the cast opens no creature picker and asks no target count or trait immunity; the range check is skipped too. **What each fixes, before and now:**

- **1, the early Spatial Awareness card** (live finding 1: "It appears when Panic Transposition's reaction is cast, before the attack resolves and before any teleport. It also appears when no teleport happens (the bearer dropped to 0 HP). The core posts it for any spell with the Teleportation trait. For Panic it should come with the actual teleport, or not at all.")
  - **Before:** `castSpell` called `teleportationArrives` at every Teleportation cast, on the user's targets or the caster. A module whose teleport comes later could not stop it, and the stamp made a later real teleport that turn fail.
  - **Now:** a registered spell is carded by its module when it moves. Every other Teleportation spell is carded at its cast, as before. The caster's own "one Teleportation action per turn" (`traitUsage.teleportation`) is still counted at the cast: casting is the action.
- **2, Spirit Arrow's picker** (live finding 3: "Its target is "one arrow or bolt", but the cast opens the core's "Select Target" dialog, which warns "no creature will be affected" unless "Continue anyway" is clicked.")
  - **Before:** `spellNeedsTarget` asked for a creature whenever the printed `targets` line was non-empty, and nothing could say the target was an arrow.
  - **Now:** a marked spell asks for none, and its cast runs with the user's targets released, then restored. Checked first: the core had no such mark (self, object or weapon); `selfApplying` says who resolves a spell, not what it targets.
- **3, Distracted's six rows** (live finding 6: "There is one row per penalty. It works, but the Effects tab is cluttered.")
  - **Before:** a `bonus` row named one slot, so the module laid six rows and the creature carried six effects.
  - **Now:** one row, one effect per spell and caster. Same-type penalties never stacked: checked for the review, `applyStacking` keeps only the worst status penalty, and no pool Distracted reaches (attack, every skill, the saves, Spellcraft) overrides the rule (`STACKING_OVERRIDES` is empty). So two spells' Distracted are two effects and still −1.
  - **Found in review:** the first build's refresh was keyed to the name alone, so any later Distracted replaced the earlier one whatever spell laid it. Bubble Buffet's Success ("until the end of its next turn") cut Animate Moustache's minute short, Bubble Buffet's ladder (`replace: "worse"`) then never laid it again, and dismissing the newer spell removed the Distracted the other still supplied. It also deleted before creating. The re-review found the same with two casters: B's Animate Moustache replaced A's, and dismissing B's stripped A's minute; the key now names the caster too.

## For Graham

- **Live finding 5** (a self-range spell refused while a token is targeted) is not changed: it is the core's behaviour from before 3.685.0, and Graham ruled on 7 November 2026 to leave it as it is.
- **Spirit Arrow, Decoherence and Freshmark copies already on a sheet** have no `targetsObject` mark, so they still open the picker until re-added from the pack. This release writes no owned item.
- **Recurring Damage's effect name** is written when the condition is laid or its value changes. A coldfire condition laid on 3.685.0 reads "Recurring Damage 16" until then.
