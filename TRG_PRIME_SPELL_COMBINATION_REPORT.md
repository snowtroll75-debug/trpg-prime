## The space

Every legal configuration of every spell was **counted** (each option 0..its limit, total within the cast cap), and the distinct mechanical paths were **generated** as cases: each option alone, at its maximum and past it, every pair, and the cap filled. The aggregator folds options one at a time, so a pair is the smallest unit in which two options can interfere; the full sweep (Warping report) checks the PP of every larger warp combination where the space allows.
