# Live test: TRG Prime 3.684.7 with Eldritch Arts 0.9.2

6 November 2026. Graham's world in Chrome, with 3.684.7 and 0.9.2 installed and active. All tests used throwaway "ZZ EA …" actors on a throwaway scene, plus a throwaway combat. Everything was cleaned up afterwards.

## Passes

**Sudden Charge:**

- **A character on 2 AP can Charge.**
  - Arming the Charge spent nothing. Its card read "Your next move is a Charge, up to 60 ft, costing 2 AP".
  - A 20 ft move toward the foe cost 2 AP (2 → 0), with the notice "Charge: 20 ft for 2 AP."
- **The Strike at the end is free.** The next turn, with the Short Sword equipped, a 20 ft Charge took AP from 3 to 1. The Strike was then offered and hit, at +4 circumstance ("Charge"), for no further AP.
- **The Strike needs a melee weapon equipped.** With no melee weapon equipped, the move was still charged, and the notice said "no equipped melee weapon to finish with".

**A cancelled Strike:** cancelled at its Versatile prompt, the Strike had spent no AP (1 before and 1 after) and posted no cards.

**Natural Leaper:**
- **The jump.** After Agile Display (AP 1 → 0, Step ready), a 5 ft Long Jump needed no check and no action. The card read "Natural Leaper: 5 feet of Long Jump — within 30 feet, no check and no action."
- **The Step.** The jump removed the Step, with the notice "the free Step is removed — it had to be … next action."

## Finding
