# TRG Prime 3.684.2

5 November 2026. A fix to the Gymnast's Agile Display, from Graham's report. Built on 3.684.1.

- **Install over 3.684.1.** No migration, and no world data changes.
- **Modules:** nothing new for modules. Eldritch Arts 0.9.1, Eldritch Arts 0.9.0 and Inner Tempest 1.0.4 run on it.

## For every world

- **Agile Display grants its Step** (Graham: "When activated, this should allow the character to step as a free (0 AP) action but does not seem to work").
  - **Before:** it charged 1 AP and rolled the check, but never granted the Step. Dragging the token afterwards cost a separate Stride or Step.
  - **Now:** the 1 AP pays for the whole action. In combat, your next drag is the Step: up to 5 ft (10 ft with Graceful Step or Tiger Stance), on your land Speed, at no AP and provoking no reactions. The card says "Step: drag the token up to N ft — no AP, no reactions provoked."
  - **It counts as a Step,** so Feather Step eases it as it eases a Step.
  - **An unused Step** ends at the start of your next turn, or when you take another basic action, as an armed Step does.
- **Agile Display asks for its creature after Use** (Graham: "the character should be able to select any target once the use ability is clicked that is within step range to target").
  - **The list:** every creature on the scene within your Step plus 5 ft, so one adjacent now or adjacent once you've stepped, nearest first. Distance is measured the way Reach is, from the nearest squares each creature occupies.
  - **It opens on your current target** if that creature is in range. Choosing a creature makes it your target, so your Strike and Flat-Footed see it.
  - **"No check — just Step"** is offered, since the book says you "may attempt" the check.
  - **Cancel spends nothing** and grants no Step. Without the AP, it says so before asking.
  - **The check and its results are unchanged:** Acrobatics against 10 + the creature's Reflex. Success or Critical makes it Flat-Footed until the end of your turn. A Blunder leaves you Prone. The Critical's Tumble Through is still a card line.
  - **A typed Reflex DC** is asked only when your character has no token on the scene.

## For Graham

- **A Strike before the Step.** The Step stays armed until your next turn or another basic action. A Strike, a spell or a feat doesn't clear it, the same as an armed Step or Charge today. So you can Strike first and still take the free Step afterwards. Should using anything else forfeit it?
